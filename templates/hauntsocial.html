What is HauntSocial?

HauntSocial is the social network for people who live for Halloween — and it lives right here on HauntMap.

You get your own page to show off your haunt, your costume, and your horror obsession:

  • 🎃 Your page — gethauntmap.com/@yourname
  • 🎵 Your anthem — the song that defines you, right on your profile
  • 👻 Your Top 8 — your favorite haunts and horror films
  • 🍬 Treats — leave comments and stickers on your friends' walls
  • 🎨 Your theme — dress your page up your way

Frank, our Frankenstein host, is everyone's first friend — he'll be waiting on your wall when you arrive.

It's free. And HauntMap stays wide open: you never need an account to find haunts, build tours, or browse events. HauntSocial is just where the haunters hang out.
