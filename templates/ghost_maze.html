    How to play

    Candy is 10 points a piece. The four power pumpkins are 50 — and for a few seconds Frank, the vampire, the skeleton and the witch turn blue and edible (200 → 400 → 800 → 1600 a monster). A candy bucket drops by twice a street for bonus points. Clear every candy to light the next street — faster monsters, braver ghost. You have three lives; don't get caught.
