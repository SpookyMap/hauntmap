✦ Any Questions ✦

Before your quest

What if I miss a display tonight?

Nothing's gone. Legends rotate from display to display all season — there's always another night, and another house.

Are Golden Nights the only way to get rares?

No — rares hide out on regular nights too. Golden Nights raise the odds, and they're when a Legendary waits at the best display in town.

Does my progress survive the season?

Yes. Your Sack, captures and badges live on your account year-round. Next season adds to your collection — it never erases it.

Can kids play without an account?

Anyone can browse the map for free. Capturing takes a free account so your Sack can save — and Young Hunter mode, coming mid-December, is the kid lane: no chat, no purchases, just the hunt.

What about my privacy?

Your location is used only at the moment you check in — never tracked while you drive, never sold. The full note is on our Privacy page.

Do I have to drive tonight?

No. Plan from the couch, build your tour, go when you feel like it. The displays are up all season.
