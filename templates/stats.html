The timer counts only while the page is the front tab — a page left open in the background earns nothing, and counting stops at 30 active minutes per page view. Views are human views: machine paths and known bot / crawler / health-check user agents are excluded (user agents are recorded since Oct 27, 2026 — earlier views can’t be screened by agent, only by path). Measured is how many of those views the timer actually timed — a page whose Measured count is far below its Views is mostly being bounced off or read without JavaScript, so trust its averages less. Avg active = total attention ÷ all views. Median active and quick exits are worked out per individual page view from the views the timer measured; a stay under about a second sends no timing at all, so the very fastest bounces don’t appear in those two columns. Pages with a high quick-exit share aren’t holding people — those are the pages to work on.
